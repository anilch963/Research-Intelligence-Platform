## Overview

Aurelia Freight Analytics, Inc. is a logistics-software company founded in 2016 in Rotterdam, Netherlands, by Mireille Vandaele and Tomasz Kowalczyk. The company sells route-optimization and freight-visibility software to mid-sized carriers and shippers in Europe and North America. Headquarters moved from Rotterdam to Amsterdam in 2021. As of the end of fiscal year 2024 the company had 412 employees across offices in Amsterdam, Warsaw and Chicago.

## Products

**RouteForge** is the company's original product: a route-optimization engine that plans multi-stop routes and reduces empty miles. Customers report an average 11% reduction in fuel costs in their first year. RouteForge pricing starts at €29 per vehicle per month.

**TraceLine** provides real-time freight visibility. It ingests position data from more than 40 ELD and telematics providers and exposes shipments through a REST API and webhooks.

**DockSync** is a dock appointment scheduling product launched in 2023. It lets shippers publish loading-dock availability and lets carriers book time slots, reducing detention time at warehouses.

The Enterprise tier of every product includes single sign-on (SSO) and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Customers and markets

Aurelia serves more than 1,150 customers. Its largest segment is regional carriers operating 50 to 500 trucks, which account for about 63% of revenue. North America contributed 31% of FY2024 revenue, up from 22% in FY2023. No single customer accounts for more than 3% of revenue.

## Key risks

Management identifies three principal risks: a prolonged freight recession that reduces carrier technology budgets; competition from established transportation management system (TMS) incumbents adding native optimization features; and tightening EU data-residency requirements that could raise hosting costs.

## Sustainability

Aurelia has committed to net-zero operations by 2030. All offices have run on 100% renewable electricity since 2022.
